# Issue 004 — Knowledge Base Normalization Pass

## Purpose

Perform a normalization and consistency review of the newly created painting critique knowledge base.

The goal is:
- improve consistency,
- reduce unnecessary duplication,
- stabilize terminology,
- and prepare the documentation for future AI-assisted retrieval and operational workflows.

This is an editorial and architectural cleanup pass.

Not a philosophical rewrite.

---

# Scope

Review all files under:

docs/knowledge-base/

---

# Goals

## 1. Detect Overlap

Identify:
- repeated doctrine,
- duplicated heuristics,
- repeated phrasing,
- overlapping sections.

Reduce redundancy where appropriate.

---

## 2. Normalize Terminology

Ensure consistent use of terms such as:
- focal hierarchy,
- value grouping,
- painterliness,
- atmospheric simplification,
- edge economy,
- chroma hierarchy,
- selective intervention,
- repaint workflow.

Avoid accidental synonym drift.

---

## 3. Improve Internal Consistency

Review:
- heading structure,
- section naming,
- tone consistency,
- formatting consistency,
- bullet hierarchy.

---

## 4. Preserve Philosophical Integrity

Do NOT:
- flatten nuance,
- shorten aggressively,
- convert into generic AI-art language,
- or remove pedagogical depth.

The project intentionally emphasizes:
- painter education,
- visual reasoning,
- and repaint-oriented learning.

---

## 5. Suggest Cross-Link Opportunities

Identify useful internal references between documents.

Examples:
- prompt_primitives ↔ visual_heuristics
- critique_patterns ↔ common_failure_modes
- watercolor_specific_doctrine ↔ multimodal_workflows

---

## 6. Identify Structural Gaps

Suggest:
- missing concepts,
- weakly covered areas,
- or opportunities for future schema extraction.

Do not implement large new sections automatically.

---

# Deliverables

## Required

- normalization review summary
- proposed edits
- implemented cleanup edits
- consistency improvements

---

## Optional

- glossary proposal
- metadata/schema proposal
- document relationship map

---

# Constraints

Preserve:
- current philosophy,
- painterly emphasis,
- and educational orientation.

Avoid:
- feature creep,
- abstraction for its own sake,
- or over-engineering.
